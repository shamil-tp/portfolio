Layover 2026: top 13 with RegretMap

Layover was an overnight hackathon organised by TinkerHub at TinkerSpace Calicut, running from the evening of 10 October to the morning of 11 October 2026. Our team built RegretMap, a community map of travel lessons with AI summaries and a chat grounded only in real traveller stories, and it was selected among the top 13 projects.

I set up the project's foundation and built the map, data layer, authentication, comments and the Gemini key pool. RegretMap is live at regret-map.shamiltp.me.

Gateways 2026: top 37 with OpsPilot

24° Shift was the international-level hackathon of Gateways 2026, the national-level inter-collegiate IT fest run by the Department of Computer Science at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bangalore. Our team built OpsPilot, an incident-response copilot that investigates outages from real evidence, proposes fixes and executes them only after a human approves, and placed in the top 37.

I built OpsPilot's FastAPI backend, deployment, CI/CD and GitHub webhooks. It is live at opspilot.shamiltp.me.
